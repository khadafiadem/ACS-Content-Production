Cipta Sehat Reborn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cipta Sehat Reborn ("Cipta Sehat", "we", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use our content production platform.

1. Information we collect

  • Account connection data. When you connect your TikTok account, we receive an access token and your TikTok user identifier (open ID) through the official TikTok OAuth authorization flow, with your consent.
  • Content data. Scripts, captions, hashtags, scheduled posting times, and the videos generated for you.
  • Usage logs. Basic technical logs (timestamps, error messages) used to operate and improve the service.

2. How we use information

  • To create, store and schedule video content you request.
  • To upload and publish content to your TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API, only after your explicit authorization and consent to publish each post.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and secure the platform.

3. Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Content is transmitted to TikTok only through TikTok's own APIs and only for the purpose you authorized.
  • We do not share your data with other third parties except where required by law.

4. Data retention

  • Tokens needed to publish content are retained until you disconnect the TikTok connection or revoke access.
  • You may request deletion of your content and account data at any time; we will remove them within a reasonable period.

5. Your choices

  • You can disconnect TikTok access at any time from the platform or through the Tiktok app settings.
  • You fully control what is posted; every post is created by your explicit action (or your own schedule settings) on your own account.

6. Security

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest where possible and transmitted only over secure connections (HTTPS).
  • Access to stored data is limited to the operator of the platform.

7. Changes & contact

We may update this policy occasionally. Questions can be directed to the operator who invited you to use the platform.
