## App Review — "Explain how each product and scope works" (English)

> This is the initial submission of our application. It is an automated content production and scheduling system operated by Apotek Cipta Sehat, a licensed Indonesian community pharmacy. It produces original, short-form (60–90 second, vertical 9:16) educational health videos in Indonesian, and publishes them only to the TikTok account that has authorized our app.
>
> Product used — **Content Posting API** (Direct Post + upload-to-inbox): Videos generated by our system are uploaded with `FILE_UPLOAD`. When the app is approved for production, approved content is directly posted (`/post/publish/video/init/`) to the connected creator's own account at the scheduled time. Until production access is granted, the same pipeline falls back to TikTok's Inbox upload (`/post/publish/inbox/video/init/`) so the creator can finish and post the draft manually. Each post is triggered only by the account owner's explicit schedule/approval on the dashboard.
>
> Scopes and how they are used:
> 1. **user.info.basic** – used once during login to load the connected account's public info so the dashboard can confirm which TikTok account is linked. No other user data is accessed.
> 2. **video.upload** – used to initialize and transfer the generated video file to TikTok (used by the Inbox/draft upload and as the media transfer step before publishing).
> 3. **video.publish** – used to directly post the finalized, reviewed video to the connected creator's account at the scheduled time.
>
> This application is a first-party internal product of Apotek Cipta Sehat: the only TikTok account that ever receives content is the pharmacy's own authorized account. We do not collect, share, or reuse third-party users' data. All content is original, AI-assisted copy is reviewed by staff before posting, every video is labelled with TikTok's AI-generated content tag when appropriate, and each script includes a disclaimer that the content is general health education and not a substitute for professional medical advice.
