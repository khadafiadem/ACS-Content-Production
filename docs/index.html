Cipta Sehat Reborn

Content production platform for short health-education videos for TikTok

About

Cipta Sehat Reborn ("Cipta Sehat") is an automated content production platform. It turns simple health topics into ready-to-post short videos: it generates the script and hook, builds narration with text-to-speech, assembles a 1080×1920 vertical video with animated captions and branded design, writes the caption and hashtags, and schedules the post.

The platform integrates with the TikTok Content Posting API so content can be uploaded and published to the TikTok account that has authorized the app.

How it works

  1. Create content — pick a health topic; the platform drafts a script, caption and hashtags (in Indonesian).
  2. Generate — narration is synthesized with text-to-speech and the vertical video is assembled automatically.
  3. Review & schedule — content can be reviewed, approved, rejected or scheduled.
  4. Publish — after TikTok Login authorization, approved content is uploaded and published to the creator's TikTok account.

Content & audience

  • Short-form educational videos about everyday health topics (sleep, nutrition, stretching, hydration, mental well-being).
  • All content is AI-assisted and labelled with AI-generated content tags on TikTok.
  • No medical advice; videos encourage viewers to consult healthcare professionals.
